Overview

Nazimabad Hospitality Concept

Karak & Crumb is an artisanal tea house and bakery concept designed by Sitely for Nazimabad, Karachi. The project explores how traditional evening chai culture can be enhanced through modern contactless ordering, instant WhatsApp dispatch, and real-time seating availability.

The Hospitality Challenge in Karachi Central

Evening dining spots in Nazimabad experience intense traffic peaks between 7:00 PM and 2:00 AM. Handwritten slips and manual order-taking frequently cause customer delays, billing mistakes, and miscommunicated custom orders. The digital experience needed to be instantaneous on low-spec smartphones, require zero application downloads, and immediately push orders to kitchen printers and customer WhatsApp receipts.

Engineering Strategy & Interface Design

Sitely engineered an ultra-lightweight web interface with zero framework bloat that loads in under 300ms on cellular data. Customers scan a table QR code, browse a visual menu with PKR pricing, customize their spiced karak blends, and tap "WhatsApp Order" to send a structured order payload directly to the counter desk with table identifier attached.
