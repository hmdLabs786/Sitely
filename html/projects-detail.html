Overview

Karachi Client Project

What the Business Needed

Fatima wanted to sell her clothing collections online instead of relying only on social media direct messages. Customers needed an easier way to browse unstitched and ready-to-wear collections, check available sizes, view clear photos, and submit orders without long back-and-forth chat delays.

What Sitely Built for the Client

A clean, fast mobile-first store showcasing seasonal dress collections with category filters (Summer Lawn, Embroidered, Formal), high-resolution image galleries, instant size selection, a simple shopping cart, and a 1-tap WhatsApp order checkout that automatically prepares order details for the seller.

4.9 / 5.0 Verified Client Review

"Website banne ke baad Instagram DMs pe rate poochne ke bajaye customers direct catalogue se select karke WhatsApp pe order bhejte hain. Hamara time bohot save ho gaya hai."

Key Practical Features

  • Mobile-optimized photo catalogue that loads quickly on cellular data.
  • Instant WhatsApp checkout with item name, size, and delivery address pre-filled.
  • Clear pricing in PKR with zero hidden surcharges or confusing checkout steps.
