Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Sitely ("we", "us", "our"), operating from Karachi, Pakistan, collects, processes, and protects personal and project information when you visit our website, communicate with our digital studio, or engage our services.

We are committed to maintaining the confidentiality and integrity of all client project specifications, technical documentation, and communications. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal or company data.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a project inquiry or contact our team, we collect the information you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Name and business contact details
  • Email address and WhatsApp phone number
  • Project scope, business details, and timeline requirements
  • Technical analytics (e.g. browser type, non-identifying telemetry) used solely to optimize site performance and accessibility

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is strictly utilized to:

  • Review your inquiry and prepare realistic project proposals

We treat all client source code, project ideas, and business information under strict confidentiality standards. When a project is completed and handed over, you own 100% of your domain, code, accounts, and customer data.
