Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Sitely ("we", "us", "our"), operating from Karachi, Pakistan, collects, processes, and protects personal and project information when you visit our website, communicate with our engineering studio, or engage our digital services.

We are committed to maintaining the confidentiality and integrity of all client project specifications, technical documentation, and communications. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal or company data.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a project inquiry or contact our team, we collect the information you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Name and job title
  • Work email address and contact details
  • Project scope, technical specifications, and timeline requirements
  • Technical analytics (e.g. browser type, non-identifying telemetry) used solely to optimize site performance and accessibility

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is strictly utilized to:

  • Review your technical inquiry and prepare architectural proposals
  • Communicate directly regarding active sprint milestones and deliverables
  • Maintain software security, prevent unauthorized access, and ensure high platform availability

3. Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure

We treat all proprietary client source code, strategic roadmaps, and business models under strict confidentiality standards. We are pleased to execute non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) prior to in-depth technical discussions.

4. Contact & Inquiries

For any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact our studio lead at habban.madani786@gmail.com.
